# AI Engineer Path (Markdown version)

*A step-by-step path from your very first AI model call to a tested, secure AI app you can put online.*

This folder has every lesson as a Markdown file. For the full clickable course with progress tracking, open `index.html` in the folder above.

## Section 1: Introduction (Week 1)

Start here. You'll see how the path works, what AI engineers actually do, and where to get help when you're stuck.

1. [How This Path Will Work](01-how-this-path-works.md) · about 30 min
2. [What AI Engineers Do](02-what-ai-engineers-do.md) · about 1.5 hours
3. [Mindset and Asking for Help](03-mindset-and-asking-for-help.md) · about 45 min

## Section 2: Foundations (Weeks 1–3)

Set up your tools and learn how language models work. You'll finish by building your first real AI feature: a poster reader.

4. [Setting Up Your Tools](04-setting-up-your-tools.md) · about 3 hours
5. [How LLMs Work](05-how-llms-work.md) · about 2 hours
6. [The AI Staircase](06-the-ai-staircase.md) · about 1.5 hours
7. [Structured Output and Errors](07-structured-output-and-errors.md) · about 4.5 hours
8. **Project:** [Poster Reader](08-project-poster-reader.md) · about 1 week

## Section 3: RAG with LLM Zoomcamp (Weeks 4–16)

Learn RAG: finding the right information and letting a model answer from it. You'll follow the free LLM Zoomcamp course and build Ask MDN, an assistant for web docs.

9. [Keyword RAG](09-keyword-rag.md) · about 1 week
10. [Tools and the Agent Loop](10-tools-and-the-agent-loop.md) · about 1 week
11. [Embeddings and Vector Search](11-embeddings-and-vector-search.md) · about 2 weeks
12. [Hybrid Search](12-hybrid-search.md) · about 3 hours
13. [ML Basics: Measuring Quality](13-ml-basics-measuring-quality.md) · about 3 hours
14. [Data Ingestion and Orchestration](14-data-ingestion-and-orchestration.md) · about 1 week
15. [Evaluation](15-evaluation.md) · about 1.5 weeks
16. [Monitoring](16-monitoring.md) · about 1.5 weeks
17. [Deploy It Safely](17-deploy-it-safely.md) · about 3 hours
18. **Project:** [Ask MDN](18-project-ask-mdn.md) · about 3 weeks

## Section 4: Agents (Weeks 17–21)

Give your app tools so it can take actions. You'll learn how agents work, build one with LangGraph, and learn to keep tools safe.

19. [Agent Fundamentals](19-agent-fundamentals.md) · about 1 week
20. [LangGraph](20-langgraph.md) · about 1.5 weeks
21. [Safe Tools](21-safe-tools.md) · about 3 hours
22. **Project:** [Give It Tools](22-project-give-it-tools.md) · about 2 weeks
23. [Start Applying](23-start-applying.md) · about 3 hours, then weekly

## Section 5: Make It Sturdy (Weeks 22–28)

Get your app ready for real users: tests that run automatically, logins, access rules, fresh data and attack tests.

24. [Evals That Catch Real Bugs](24-evals-that-catch-real-bugs.md) · about 1 week
25. [Testing and CI](25-testing-and-ci.md) · about 1 week
26. [Login and Access Rules](26-login-and-access-rules.md) · about 1 week
27. [Fresh Data](27-fresh-data.md) · about 3 hours
28. [Red-Team Your App](28-red-team-your-app.md) · about 3 hours
29. **Project:** [Make It Sturdy](29-project-make-it-sturdy.md) · about 3 weeks

## Section 6: Conclusion (Weeks 29–30)

Catch up, polish your projects and plan what you'll build next.

30. [What's Next](30-whats-next.md) · about 2 weeks (spare)
