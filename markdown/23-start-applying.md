# Start Applying

*Section 4: Agents · Lesson 23 of 30 · about 3 hours, then weekly*

## Introduction

You now have projects you can show and explain. That's enough to start applying for jobs, so don't wait until the end of the path.

## Lesson overview

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Know what AI engineering interviews look like.
- Tell clear stories about your projects.
- Keep a steady weekly routine for applications.

## Assignment

1. Read the interview and take-home sections of the [AI Engineering Field Guide](https://github.com/alexeygrigorev/ai-engineering-field-guide). Try one take-home task with a time limit.
2. Add Poster Reader and Ask MDN to your resume ([resume guide](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/resume/)). Lead with the numbers from your READMEs.
3. Prepare three short stories from your log: a hard bug, a design choice and a mistake you fixed ([behavioral interview guide](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/behavioral-interview/)).
4. Practise explaining how Ask MDN works, out loud, in 3 minutes.
5. From now on, apply to 5 jobs a week while you keep going with the path.

## Knowledge check

Use these questions to check what you learned. If one stumps you, follow its link and review. You don't need to memorize anything.

- [Which project numbers belong on your resume?](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/resume/)
- [What makes a good story about a mistake?](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/behavioral-interview/)
