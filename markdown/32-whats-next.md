# What's Next

*Section 6: Conclusion · Lesson 32 of 32 · about 2 weeks (spare)*

## Introduction

> **You started by calling a model. Now you can ship one that keeps secrets.**

You made it! You can now design, build, test, secure and ship an AI app, and explain every part of it. That's a big achievement.

Use these two weeks to catch up, polish your work and plan your Campus Assistant.

**After this, you can** plan a product from skills you've already proven.

**The skill that lasts:** scoping, and saying no to features.

## Lesson overview

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Plan the Campus Assistant using what you built.
- Choose one deep dive, but only if a job you want asks for it.
- Keep your job application routine going.

## Assignment

1. Finish anything you skipped. Then read back through your log and write one page: what you can build now that you couldn't at the start.
2. Plan the Campus Assistant on one page, starting from the feature list you made in [The AI Staircase](06-the-ai-staircase.md). Match each feature to something you've already built: login, row-level security, hybrid search, approvals, evals, CI and attack tests.
3. Pick at most one deep dive, and only if a job you want asks for it: fine-tuning ([mlabonne's LLM course](https://github.com/mlabonne/llm-course)), how models work inside ([LLMs from Scratch](https://github.com/rasbt/LLMs-from-scratch)), or coding agents ([Hugging Face Context Course](https://huggingface.co/learn/context-course/unit0/introduction)).
4. Keep your weekly job application block going, and make the first Campus Assistant commit within 7 days. Momentum is easier to keep than to restart.

### Recall · 10 min

5. Close this page. In your log, answer from memory, then check:
