# What's Next

*Section 6: Conclusion · Lesson 30 of 30 · about 2 weeks (spare)*

## Introduction

You made it! You can now design, build, test, secure and ship an AI app, and explain every part of it. That's a big achievement.

Use these two weeks to catch up, polish your work and plan your next build.

## Lesson overview

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Plan the Campus Assistant using what you built.
- Choose one deep dive, but only if a job you want asks for it.
- Keep your job application routine going.

## Assignment

1. Finish anything you skipped, then polish your three project READMEs.
2. Plan the Campus Assistant on one page. Match each university feature to something you've already built: login, row-level security, hybrid search, approvals, evals, CI and attack tests. If you have the book, read chapter 10, “AI Engineering Architecture and User Feedback”.
3. Pick at most one deep dive from the list below, and only if a job you want asks for it.
4. Keep applying to 5 jobs a week.
