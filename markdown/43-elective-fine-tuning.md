# Elective: Fine-Tune a Small Model

*Section 8: Electives · Elective 2 of 5 · about 1.5 weeks*

## Introduction

> **Fine-tune last, and only when you can measure the win.**

Fine-tune a small open model with LoRA for one narrow job, such as turning Banglish questions into clean English search queries, and compare it fairly with prompting.

**After this, you can** fine-tune a small model with LoRA and prove whether it beats prompting.

**The skill that lasts:** deciding when training is worth it, with evidence instead of hype.

## Lesson overview

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Build a train/validation/test set from your approved data.
- Fine-tune with LoRA on a free or cheap GPU.
- Compare against your prompt-based baseline on the same test set.

## Assignment

1. Build 500–1,000 pairs (Banglish question → English search query): your curator-written paraphrases plus a labelled share generated by an LLM. Split by original question, so each question and all its paraphrases sit on one side; every intent still appears in training. The test set uses only real, human-written questions.
2. Fine-tune a small open model with LoRA in a free notebook GPU, following the [PEFT documentation](https://huggingface.co/docs/peft/index). Record time, cost and settings.
3. Compare with your rewrite prompt on the test set: accuracy, latency, cost. Write whether you'd ship it, and what would change your mind.
